About Me

Computer Engineer based in NYC. I specialize in the moment where code meets copper and silicone.

There is a specific moment in engineering that never gets old: the first time the firmware successfully toggles a pin and the hardware responds. That instant where a digital command becomes a physical action is why I chose Computer Engineering.

I earned my B.E. from The City College of New York (CCNY) in 2020. As the Team Lead for a group of four, I headed the development of our senior capstone—an eBike Conversion Kit. We focused on bringing advanced safety features like LiDAR to the micro-mobility market, an homage to the "future-forward" tech I grew up admiring in systems like K.I.T.T. from Knight Rider.

Currently, I am a Software Engineer (Level II) at Clarivate Analytics. While my professional focus is on high-level software architecture, I keep my hardware skills sharp through personal builds and custom PCB design.

Outside of the IDE, I’m usually tinkering with my cars or spending time with my German Shepherd. Whether it’s troubleshooting a mechanical issue in a garage or routing a PCB at a desk, the goal is always the same: building systems that work.
